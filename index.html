About

About Para Village

A peaceful village surrounded by rivers, green fields, and generations of memories.

A Living Story

Para is more than a village. It is a place where families have lived together for centuries, preserving traditions through farming, festivals, education, and community life.

The river has always been the heart of the village, bringing people together and supporting everyday life.

  • Founded: 300+ Years Ago
  • Landscape: River, Fields & Hills
  • Population: 3,240+
  • Main Occupation: Farming & Handicrafts
