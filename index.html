About

About Para Village

A peaceful village surrounded by rivers, green fields, and generations of memories.

A Living Story

Para is more than a village. It is a place where families have lived together for centuries, preserving traditions through farming, festivals, education, and community life.

The river has always been the heart of the village, bringing people together and supporting everyday life.

  • Founded300+ Years Ago
  • LandscapeRiver, Fields & Hills
  • Population3,240+
  • Main OccupationFarming & Handicrafts

History

Our History

Discover the journey of Para Village through the centuries.

1724

Village Founded

The first families settled along the river and began cultivating fertile land.

1850

Market Established

The local market became an important center for trade between neighboring villages.

1952

Village School Opened

Education became an essential part of community life, opening doors for future generations.

1998

Community Development

Roads, irrigation, and public spaces were improved, connecting Para to the wider world.
